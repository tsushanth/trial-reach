# Progression: source and definition dependence (consortium curated lung cohort)

Aggregates only. Data used under the owner's access terms (aggregate results only; cite the consortium; include the required
acknowledgement and logo in any publication or talk). Cohort size 1,846 patients at four institutions, 35,101 curated
imaging scans and 24,909 oncologist assessments (one per month curated).

- First progression within 30 days between imaging-derived and oncologist-note-derived dates: **33.0%** of 1,041 patients
  with progression in both. Imaging earlier by more than 30 days: 52.9%; note earlier: 14.0%; median difference 39 days.
  Similar at each institution (30.7 to 38.8%).
- Median progression-free survival in the same 1,380 patients by the four official definitions: imaging 4.9 months;
  oncologist 7.8; imaging OR oncologist 3.4; imaging AND oncologist 9.4. Same ordering at every institution.
- Labels are not absorbing: only 25.3% of scans after a first imaging progression are labelled progressing.
- Caveats: note dates are visit dates; progression-free survival starts at diagnosis or first distant metastasis, not
  therapy start; curated labels contain errors.
- **Novelty (checked 2026-10-22): the main claim is not new.** The data's authors reported that radiologist-based and
  oncologist-based progression-free survival differ and recommended specifying the definition (2020 abstract, DOI
  10.1200/JCO.2020.38.15_suppl.9622). Possibly incremental: the full cohort, the and/or definitions, and date-level agreement.
